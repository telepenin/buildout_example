## What differs between models

- **Case size (43 / 47 / 51 mm)** — drives display size, battery, and weight. 43 mm only exists in the standard Fenix 8 AMOLED.
- **Display tech**:
  - **AMOLED** — vivid, full‑color, default for most buyers.
  - **MIP Solar** — dimmer transflective screen, but solar charging extends runtime massively. Only on Fenix 8 Solar (47 / 51 mm).
  - **MicroLED** — exclusive to the Fenix 8 Pro 51 mm; brightest display Garmin makes (industry‑leading nits) at a steep price premium.
- **Materials** — every Fenix 8 uses a polymer main case, but the **bezel/back/lens** vary: stainless steel + Gorilla Glass (cheapest, AMOLED only), titanium + sapphire (Sapphire editions), or titanium + DLC coating + sapphire (premium).
- **Battery life** scales roughly linearly with case size; Solar adds the most when used outdoors.
- **Connectivity (Pro only)** — LTE cellular, Skylo satellite messaging, inReach SOS, and phone‑free LiveTrack. Requires inReach subscription.
- **Price delta** — the standard Fenix 8 spans **$200** ($999.99 → $1,199.99). Stepping up to the Pro adds **$100–$800** depending on display tech.

## Shared across the entire Fenix 8 series

- Elevate Gen 5 HR sensor, ECG, Pulse Ox, sleep tracking
- Built‑in LED flashlight, speaker, microphone (calls + voice commands)
- Multi‑band GPS with SatIQ, preloaded TopoActive maps, dynamic round‑trip routing
- 40 m dive rating (depth gauge, dive apps)
- Garmin Pay, music storage, Connect IQ apps
