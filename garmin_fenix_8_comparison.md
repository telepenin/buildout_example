# Garmin Fenix 8 Series — Sapphire Edition Comparison

Filtered to **Sapphire crystal lens** models only (Titanium bezel + sapphire lens, or DLC Titanium + sapphire). The cheaper Stainless Steel + Gorilla Glass DX variants are excluded — sapphire is the recommended choice for adventure use because it's ~Mohs 9 hardness and almost scratch‑proof in normal wear.

## Why Sapphire over Gorilla Glass DX

| Property | Gorilla Glass DX | **Sapphire crystal** |
|---|---|---|
| Hardness (Mohs) | ~6.5–7 | **~9** |
| Scratch resistance | Good (vulnerable to sand, granite, steel) | Practically scratch‑proof |
| Shatter resistance on flat impact | Slightly better | Slightly more brittle on point impacts |
| Optical clarity | Slightly less reflective | Excellent, marginally more reflective |
| Bundled bezel | Stainless steel | **Titanium** (lighter + corrosion‑proof) |
| Price premium | — | **+ ~$100** over the steel variant |

## How to choose within the Sapphire lineup

- **Smallest wrist / lightest watch** → Fenix 8 43 mm AMOLED Sapphire ($1,099.99). Only Sapphire size under 47 mm.
- **Best balance of size, battery, and price** → Fenix 8 47 mm AMOLED Sapphire ($1,199.99). The "default" Fenix.
- **Maximum battery, no Pro features** → Fenix 8 51 mm Solar Sapphire ($1,199.99). 37–48 days smartwatch with sun.
- **Bright AMOLED + biggest battery** → Fenix 8 51 mm AMOLED Sapphire ($1,299.99).
- **Need phone‑free safety / messaging** → Fenix 8 Pro 47 mm or 51 mm ($1,199.99–$1,299.99) + inReach subscription.
- **Money is no object, want the brightest display Garmin makes** → Fenix 8 Pro 51 mm MicroLED ($1,999).

## Shared across the entire Sapphire lineup

- Sapphire crystal lens + titanium bezel/back (DLC titanium on most variants)
- Elevate Gen 5 HR sensor, ECG, Pulse Ox, sleep tracking
- Built‑in LED flashlight, speaker, microphone (calls + voice commands)
- Multi‑band GPS with SatIQ, preloaded TopoActive maps, dynamic round‑trip routing
- 40 m dive rating (depth gauge, dive apps)
- Garmin Pay, music storage, Connect IQ apps
